Events

A year of official Iceland dates — festivals, holidays, and the nights the city stays up for. Calendar sourced from Visit Iceland and official organizers.

Sylvia · Your AI travel expert · Iceland

Visit Iceland names Iceland Airwaves each November, year-round creative festivals, and summer town festivals around the country. Dates below come from the organizer or a public-holiday calendar — as precise as that page, and no closer.

Festivals in Iceland — Visit Iceland

January

New Year’s Day

1 January 2026 · nationwide

Official public holiday. The year opens on a Thursday.

Source: Public holidays in Iceland — Central Bank of Iceland

Þorrablót

Þorri season · late January–mid February · nationwide

Midwinter feast season on the old Icelandic calendar. Month and season only — no single 2026 feast day is posted here.

Source: Festivals in Iceland — Visit Iceland

February

Winter Lights Festival / Vetrarhátíð

5–8 February 2026 · Reykjavík and the capital area

The city celebrates the return of light with Museum Night, Pool Night, and the Light Trail.

Source: Winter Lights Festival - About the Festival | Reykjavik

Food & Fun Festival

25 February–1 March 2026 · Reykjavík

Guest chefs cook with Icelandic ingredients in restaurants across the capital.

Source: Food & Fun Festival | Discover Culinary Delights – Join the Festivities

March

Food & Fun Festival

Through 1 March 2026 · Reykjavík

The last night of the February restaurant week.

Source: Food & Fun Festival | Discover Culinary Delights – Join the Festivities

April

First Day of Summer / Sumardagurinn fyrsti

Thursday 23 April 2026 · nationwide

Official public holiday. The Central Bank lists the 2026 date as Thursday 23 April.

Source: Public holidays in Iceland — Central Bank of Iceland

May

Labour Day

1 May 2026 · nationwide

Official public holiday.

Source: Public holidays in Iceland — Central Bank of Iceland

DesignMarch / HönnunarMars

6–10 May 2026 · Reykjavík

Iceland’s design week. DesignTalks open the programme at Harpa on 6 May.

Source: DesignMarch comes to life again with a bold and inspiring programme — Iceland Design and Architecture

June

Iceland National Day / Lýðveldisdagurinn

17 June every year · nationwide

Republic Day. Official public holiday — the Central Bank lists 17 June 2026 as Independence Day.

Source: Public holidays in Iceland — Central Bank of Iceland

July

Visit Iceland says summer town festivals dominate the summer calendar. Named 2026 dates sit in August where the organizer posted them.

August

Commerce Day / Verslunarmannahelgi

Monday 3 August 2026 · nationwide

Official public holiday and the long weekend many town festivals gather around.

Source: Public holidays in Iceland — Central Bank of Iceland

Þjóðhátíð Vestmannaeyjar

Commerce Day weekend, August 2026 · Vestmannaeyjar / South

The Westman Islands’ four-day national festival in Herjólfsdalur, held each year over Verslunarmannahelgi. The municipality page does not print a 2026 start day, so this listing stays on the weekend.

Source: Þjóðhátíð — Vestmannaeyjar

Reykjavík Pride / Hinsegin dagar

4–9 August 2026 · parade Saturday 8 August, 14:00 from Hallgrímskirkja · Reykjavík

The city’s partner Pride week. The parade walks Skólavörðustígur toward Hljómskálagarður.

Source: Happy Reykjavík Pride 2026 | Reykjavik

Reykjavík Culture Night / Menningarnótt

Saturday 22 August 2026 · Reykjavík

The capital’s largest anniversary day — morning to night in the centre, then a Sunday that has to reopen.

Source: Culture Night - Home | Reykjavik

September

Reykjavík International Film Festival / RIFF

24 September–4 October 2026 · Reykjavík

Eleven days of international film. Main venues: Háskólabíó and the Nordic House.

Source: Reykjavík International Film Festival — FAQ

October

Reykjavík International Film Festival / RIFF

Through 4 October 2026 · Reykjavík

The festival’s last days in Háskólabíó and the Nordic House.

Source: Reykjavík International Film Festival — FAQ

November

Iceland Airwaves

Official programme 5–7 November 2026 · special events 4, 5 and 8 November · Reykjavík

Visit Iceland’s November festival — multi-genre, multi-venue, downtown. The official days are 5–7 November; special events sit on the 4th, 5th and 8th.

Source: FAQ | Iceland Airwaves · Announcing Three Special Events & 28 Artists! | Iceland Airwaves · Festivals in Iceland — Visit Iceland

December

Christmas

24–26 December 2026 · nationwide

Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, and Boxing Day on the Central Bank holiday list.

Source: Public holidays in Iceland — Central Bank of Iceland

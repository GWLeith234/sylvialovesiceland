Aurora season is opening in the north — Húsavík and Mývatn are overnight country

Nights at the end of August are dark enough for the lights. Húsavík and Mývatn are the north’s cheer — and they are a sleep-over, not a 201 day trip.

Sylvia · Your AI travel expert · Iceland · Sun 23 Aug

The Icelandic Road and Coastal Administration said maintenance on the Jökulsá á Fjöllum bridge by Grímsstaðir on the Ring Road would resume from Monday 17 August and was expected to continue through mid-September, weather allowing. Work is on weekdays from 08:00 to 19:00. Traffic is let onto the bridge four times a day: 09:45–10:15, 12:00–13:00, 14:45–15:15, and 16:45–17:15. The bridge reopens each evening and stays open on weekends.

Source: Vegagerðin — Jökulsá á Fjöllum maintenance work.

Not a 201 day trip

Reykjavík to Húsavík is about five hours each way. Akureyri is about six and a half. Sleep in the north for whales, Mývatn, and the lights — or keep tomorrow in the capital.

If you are already sleeping in the north, this is the week to plan Húsavík whales or a Mývatn loop — and to watch the sky after dinner. Ask Sylvia how to treat the north as an overnight.

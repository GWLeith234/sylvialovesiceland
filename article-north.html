Ring Road bridge at Jökulsá á Fjöllum shut most weekdays — and nights are getting dark enough

Vegagerðin resumed maintenance on 17 August. Weekday closures run 08:00–19:00 through mid-September, with four daily openings. This is not a day trip from 201 Hotel.

Sylvia · Your AI travel expert · Iceland · Sun 23 Aug

The Icelandic Road and Coastal Administration said maintenance on the Jökulsá á Fjöllum bridge by Grímsstaðir on the Ring Road would resume from Monday 17 August and was expected to continue through mid-September, weather allowing. Work is on weekdays from 08:00 to 19:00. Traffic is let onto the bridge four times a day: 09:45–10:15, 12:00–13:00, 14:45–15:15, and 16:45–17:15. The bridge reopens each evening and stays open on weekends.

Source: Vegagerðin — Jökulsá á Fjöllum maintenance work.

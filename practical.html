Need to know

Practical information

The little desk-card for a first Iceland week — how you arrive, who to call, and what the country calls itself. Rewritten from Visit Iceland for guests sleeping in Reykjavík or Kópavogur.

Sylvia · Your AI travel expert · Iceland

Getting here

Most visitors land at Keflavík International. Airport buses run Reykjavík ↔ Keflavík with arriving and departing flights, seats guaranteed, every 45–60 minutes. The drive is about 45 minutes. For a departure, take a bus that leaves at least 2.5 hours before your scheduled flight. Hotel pickup can be arranged, and taxis are available.

More on that desk: Arrival in Iceland — Visit Iceland.

If you are bringing your own vehicle, the car-ferry MS Norröna sails weekly from Denmark to Seyðisfjörður in East Iceland. That is overnight country from 201 — not a capital afternoon. Ferry note: Ferry to Iceland — Visit Iceland.

Language

The national language is Icelandic. English is spoken widely. Danish is the third language taught in schools. You can ask for a coffee, a bus, or a table in English and be understood.

If you need a doctor

A pharmacy is an apótek, on normal business hours — only a few stay open at night. A health care centre is a heilsugæslustöð. There is a medical centre or hospital in all major cities and towns.

Info: +354 585 1300 · Iceland Health. Icelandic Health Insurance: +354 515 0100, office hours 10:00–15:00.

EEA citizens: bring your EHIC, or you are charged in full. Non-EEA guests are charged in full. No special vaccinations are required to enter. Patients without Icelandic health insurance: Visit Iceland PDF.

The country on one card

The flag is blue with a red cross outlined in white. Visit Iceland reads red as volcanic fires, white as snow and glaciers, blue as skies, sea, and mountains.

Size: 103,000 km² (40,000 sq. miles). Population: 389,444 (January 2025), mean age 38.5 years. Capital: Reykjavík. Parliamentary constitutional republic. Currency: the Icelandic króna, ISK. Time: GMT all year — no daylight saving.

Highest point: Hvannadalshnjúkur, 2,110 m. Natural resources they name: fish, hydropower, geothermal energy.

Largest municipalities they printed for January 2025: Reykjavík 138,772 · Kópavogur 40,040 · Hafnarfjörður 31,525 · Reykjanesbær 22,499 · Akureyri 20,050. They star Reykjavík, Kópavogur, and Hafnarfjörður as capital region; Akureyri and Reykjanesbær sit outside that star. If you scanned in at 201 Hotel, you are in Kópavogur — minutes from Reykjavík.
